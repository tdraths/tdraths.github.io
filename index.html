About

I'm Tom — co-founder of WD O'Connell, an Irish whiskey merchant, where I've spent the past several years running digital operations, brand development, and product launches. Alongside that, I build the tools the business runs on, including Copper & Quill, a custom ERP.

I'm currently training as an ATEX inspector with AVS in Dungarvan, and building the inspection tooling featured on this site to close gaps I've run into on site. I'm drawn to work where process, tooling, and hands-on technical inspection meet — particularly project controls and operations roles.

Outside of that, I coach youth soccer and hurling, and keep a running interest in sports data and analytics — most recently a hand-coded open dataset from hurling broadcasts.
